About the laboratory

Engineering antennas and systems that connect, sense and power.

The Antenna/Microwave Research Lab at IIT Ropar works across antenna engineering, wireless power and data transfer, RF energy harvesting, near-field field-forming, and communication systems. The group combines electromagnetic design, circuit and system development, and intelligent methods for emerging IoT, 5G/6G, mobility, and sensing applications.
